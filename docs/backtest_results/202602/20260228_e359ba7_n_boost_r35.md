## CW-Tier Bias Audit

### Lookahead Bias: ✅ NONE FOUND

- **E2** (SMA proximity): SMA(20) at bar i uses close[i-19:i+1] → all past/current
- **C2** (BTC neutral): btc_mom_8 at 15m bar i*ratio → past data
- **B3** (Confluence): sig_d/sig_s at bar i from VR[i] → backward-looking
- **A3** (Quality+): VR[i] or sq_dur[i] → backward-looking
- **Score weighting**: Applied at ENTRY bar, not EXIT

### Survivorship Bias: MILD, SHARED

- 50 symbols (hardcoded whitelist), 9 with <50% of max bars
- Short-lived: TAOUSDT (31%), ENAUSDT (32%), TONUSDT (33%), WIFUSDT (35%), WLDUSDT (43%), PEPE (47%), SUI (47%), ARB (49%), STX (50%)
- Delisted coins (LUNA, FTT) not in whitelist → mild bias
- **Shared equally** across baseline and all variants → no differential advantage

## Key Findings

### 1. N-Boosting Frontier: Clear EV–N Trade-off

Every N-boosting lever degrades per-trade EV. The marginal trades opened by relaxing thresholds have **lower signal quality**.

| Approach | N Boost | EV Drop | Trade-off |
|----------|---------|---------|-----------|
| VR +0.05 (D only) | +85% | -25bp | Moderate |
| VR +0.05 (S only) | +131% | -31bp | Poor (DD 56%) |
| Duration 8→6 | +28% | -8bp | **Best single lever** |
| 4th TF (30m) | +17% | -5bp | **Best quality** |
| VR+Dur combo (F1) | +289% | -38bp | Max N with edge |
| Full boost (F4) | +427% | -40bp | Edge barely positive |

### 2. Best N-Booster: B1-Dur6 (Duration Relaxation)

- **N**: 900 (+28% from 702)
- **EV**: +56.6bp (only -7.5bp from baseline)
- **PF**: 1.483, **t**: 5.60, **DD**: 24.9%
- **EV×N**: +50,940 (+13.2%)
- Minimal EV degradation for meaningful N increase

### 3. Best Quality N-Booster: E1-4TF (Add 30m)

- **N**: 824 (+17%)
- **EV**: +58.9bp (only -5.2bp)
- **PF**: 1.531, **t**: 5.71, **DD**: 22.5%
- EV×N: +48,534 (+7.9%)
- **Lowest DD among all N-boosters**

### 4. CW-Tier Dominates EV×N Ranking (But DD Is Extreme)

Top 3 OOS EV×N are all CW variants (G4, G2, G3) with DD=65–73%. The CW sizing lever is powerful but **doubles drawdown risk**. For deployment, position sizing must be capped.

### 5. Conservative N-Boost: A1-D50 (D VR Only)

- **N**: 1,300 (+85%)
- **EV**: +39.4bp
- **PF**: 1.405, **DD**: 24.5%
- Non-CW, reasonable risk profile
- **EV×N**: +51,220 (+13.8%)

### 6. Aggressive Combos Destroy Edge

F3-Full and F2-VR+Dur+Hold push N to 3,000+ but EV drops to ~20bp with DD >50%. The edge becomes too thin for practical use after fees/slippage.

## Recommended Actions

1. **B1-Dur6**: Best risk-adjusted N-boost. Implement `sq_dur ≥ 6` for S_d8 signals. +28% trades, -7.5bp EV, DD manageable at 24.9%.

2. **E1-4TF**: If 30m resampling infrastructure is ready, add 30m as 4th TF. +17% trades, -5.2bp EV, lowest DD at 22.5%.

3. **CW-Tier with DD cap**: Use G1-CW-Base sizing BUT cap max weight at 2.0x instead of 2.5x, and/or reduce base position size to keep effective DD <25%.

4. **Do NOT deploy F1+ combos**: VR relaxation to 0.50/0.60 + duration relaxation overwhelms dedup and opens too many marginal trades (DD >50%).
