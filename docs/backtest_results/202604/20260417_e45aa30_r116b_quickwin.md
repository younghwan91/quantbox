## 2. Compute Metrics Fix (Prerequisite)

Resolved pre-existing bug in `quantbox/backtest/trade_sim.py::compute_metrics()`:

- **Root cause:** `cumprod(1.0 + pnl)` 1x-compound model exploded for high-N × high-per-trade-rate strategies (Donchian reported Calmar=61M, ann_ret_pct=1.76B%).
- **Fix:** Added optional `portfolio_size: int = 1` parameter. When >1, scales per-trade PnL by 1/portfolio_size before compounding (models realistic multi-slot capital allocation). Default=1 preserves backward compatibility for r41_r65 research scripts.
- **Tests:** `tests/test_compute_metrics.py` — 11 new tests covering backward compat, portfolio scaling, edge cases, and baseline regression. Existing `tests/test_trade_sim.py::TestComputeMetrics` 8/8 still pass.

**Revised baseline** (at `portfolio_size=10`):

| Strategy | N | WR% | PF | MDD% | Sharpe | Calmar | ann_ret% |
|----------|---|-----|----|------|--------|--------|----------|
| D_mom8 | 1051 | 43.9 | 1.092 | 2.9 | 0.752 | 0.9 | 2.5 |
| MA Cluster | 1576 | 68.5 | 1.834 | 3.5 | 4.853 | 11.5 | 40.0 |
| Donchian | 1876 | 58.3 | 4.304 | 3.3 | 11.275 | 147.8 | 491.7 |

Original baseline report had MDD 28-30% (1x-compound) and absurd Calmar for Donchian. New values reflect 10-slot portfolio reality; MDD is ~10x smaller (2.9-3.5%) matching live-observed DD of 2.1%.

**Score distribution over 1079 raw trades:** score=0 (81), score=1 (406), score=2 (592), score=3 (0).

**Finding:** Higher conviction thresholds DEGRADE PF. Rationale hypothesis: score-2 conditions (E2 close-to-SMA + C2 BTC-neutral + A3 VR<0.35) correlate with consolidation/indecision setups that fail to break out. The "quality filter" is anti-signal for D_mom8.

**Implication:** The `VS_MIN_CONVICTION_SCORE=5` pause flag (in `.env` since 2026-04-17) can be fully removed — conviction gate alone is not part of the re-enable config.

## 10. Caveats & Follow-ups

### Caveats

- **Survivorship bias risk.** Blacklist is derived from 2024-01 → 2026-04 sample. Symbols that lost in that period may not lose forever (market regime shifts). Mitigation: re-run baseline + this sweep quarterly; rotate blacklist based on fresh evidence.
- **Reduced trade count (40%).** With fewer trades, statistical power of forward PF estimate drops. Confidence interval on PF 1.362 is wider than on PF 1.092.
- **b3_confluence still reserved.** If a future r117/r118 implements b3, conviction math will change and this sweep is outdated.
- **Donchian PF 4.304 still suspicious.** High but internally consistent; live Donchian performance should be monitored for backtest-live gap.

### Follow-ups (not in r116b scope)

1. **US-R116B-NEW1:** Implement `d_symbol_blacklist` config field + gate in `strategy.py`. Unit tests. Pre-commit parity.
