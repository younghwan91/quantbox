## Applied Changes

Only MA Cluster params changed (VR validated as near-optimal):

| Parameter | Before | After | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|
| `ma_cluster_min_momentum` | 0.003 (hardcoded) | **0.010** (configurable) | Filter weak momentum signals (+22% EV) |
| `ma_cluster_max_duration` | ∞ (none) | **24 bars** (configurable) | Prevent zombie clusters (e.g., duration=70 TRXUSDT) |

VR Squeeze params **unchanged**: `d_vr=0.50`, `d_sl=0.01`, `d_tp=0.05` (trailing stop handles effective TP).

> **Note**: Top combined rank uses VR sl=0.8% instead of current 1.0%. The VR-only sweep shows
> sl=1.0% (Calmar=8.7) slightly outperforms sl=0.8% (Calmar=8.5) when evaluated standalone.
> In combined portfolio, sl=0.8% produces marginally better Calmar (21.0 vs 18.8) due to
> larger trade count complementing MA Cluster. This is a potential future optimization.
