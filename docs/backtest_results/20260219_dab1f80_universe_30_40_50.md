## Key Analysis

### B-50 wins across all metrics:

1. **+36.6% more return** vs B-30 ($144,808 vs $106,538)
2. **Better Sharpe** (7.24 vs 7.09) — wider universe = more opportunity, not just dilution
3. **Lower max DD** (9.1% vs 9.7%) — diversification benefit realized
4. **Calmar 751** vs 512 — massively better risk-adjusted
5. **Consistent yearly**: B-50 wins all 3 years, particularly strong in Y2/Y3
6. **Trade quality maintained**: avg PnL/trade +1.281% (highest of all variants)

### Why B-40 has higher DD (13.7%):

B-40 shows a 13.7% max DD spike in Year 1 that B-50 avoids. The additional 10 T4 symbols in B-50 provide enough diversification to offset concentrated drawdown events.

### Scaling behavior:

- B-30 → B-40: +20.9% return, +22% more trades/day (proportional)
- B-40 → B-50: +13.0% return, +12% more trades/day (proportional)
- Trade quality (avg PnL/trade) stays flat: ~+1.28% across all variants

## Winner: **B-50** (50 symbols, 2 slots)

B-50 dominates in return, Sharpe, drawdown, and Calmar ratio. The additional mid-cap symbols contribute meaningful signal volume without degrading trade quality; this confirms that our strategy alpha is not just concentrated in top-10 coins.

## Recommendation

Adopt 50-symbol universe for production. The T3+T4 symbols have proven 3-year track records and provide genuine additional alpha.
